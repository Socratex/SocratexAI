# Decision record

Write one only for a choice future work must not reverse by accident. Skip small choices.

- Decision: one line.
- Context: what forced the choice.
- Why this one: and what was rejected.
- Consequences: what is now fixed or harder.
- Revisit when: the condition that reopens it.
